About the Artist

Painting Stories Through Light

I am McDonald Simon, a Tanzanian oil painter whose work explores memory, light and the quiet dignity found in everyday life. Working primarily in oils, I combine impressionistic brushwork with carefully observed realism to create paintings that invite viewers to pause and reflect. Each piece is painted by hand and exists as an original work.
