About The Artist

Creating Timeless Visual Stories

My name is McDonald Simon, a Tanzanian Visual Artist whose work explores memory, atmosphere and the emotional power of light. Rather than simply recording appearances, I seek to create artworks that invite viewers to pause, reflect and discover stories within each brushstroke. Working across multiple traditional media, every artwork is approached with patience, craftsmanship and respect for timeless artistic principles.
