About

McDonald Simon

Visual Artist

Based in Tanzania, my work explores light, memory, atmosphere and the stories that quietly shape our lives. Rather than simply painting subjects, I seek to preserve moments that invite people to pause, reflect and connect. Although many of my recent works are created in oil, my practice also embraces graphite, pen, watercolor and acrylic, allowing each idea to find its most natural expression.

"Every artwork begins with silence before it begins with colour."
