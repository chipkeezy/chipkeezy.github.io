About the Artist

McDonald Simon

I am a Tanzanian visual artist specializing in oil painting, blending impressionism with realism to create emotionally rich narratives. My work explores memory, light, culture, and everyday human experiences. Each painting is an attempt to transform ordinary moments into timeless stories through color, atmosphere, and brushwork.
